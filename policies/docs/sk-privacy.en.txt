SK Privacy Policy
Established: September 25, 2026

Article 1 Basic Policy
1. SK (the "Developer") handles Users' information in the Services defined in the SK Terms of Service in accordance with this Policy.
2. The Developer handles information only to the extent necessary to provide the Services. The information handled is never used for advertising, sale, provision to third parties, or tracking behavior outside the Services. The access analytics in Article 4-2 is performed only for people who allow it.
3. Details of how information is handled in Y-FILTER., SK Hub Systems, and the SK Hub Systems account are set out in the "Y-FILTER. Terms of Service and Privacy Policy," the "SK Hub Systems Terms of Service," and the "SK Hub Systems Account Terms." Where they differ from this Policy, their provisions prevail for that service.

Article 2 Overview by Service
(a) The Site (including SK's Lab tools), MALU, Nagi, and the Y-FILTER. management console: handle the access check information in Article 3. MALU's search terms, search history, and settings are stored only in the browser and never sent. However, when signed in with an SK Hub Systems account, as set out in the SK Hub Systems Account Terms, MALU settings are synced, words saved to the wordbook are stored in the user's own account when connected to MALU, and search history is stored there when search history sync is turned on.
(b) Y-FILTER.: filtering happens inside the browser, and browsing history and search terms are never sent to the Developer.
(c) SK Hub Systems: stores settings, device status, settings backups, and the like only when Y-FILTER.'s remote management is used or Y-FILTER. is connected to an SK Hub Systems account as a personal device.
(d) Contact: stores what is entered in the contact form in SK Hub Systems.
(e) SK Hub Systems account: only when an account is created, stores the information of the account used to sign in (Google, GitHub, LINE, or Kakao) (ID, name, profile picture, and for Google and GitHub, email address; no email address is received from LINE or Kakao; if a passkey is registered, its public key and related data, but no fingerprint, face, or similar information) and the date the terms were agreed to, among other things. The time zone (approximate region) and support ID of the browsers used while signed in are also stored as usage records.
(f) Nagi: focus records (when you started focusing and how long you focused) and settings are stored only in the browser and are not sent. However, when you connect Nagi to your SK Hub Systems account, your focus records and daily goal are stored in your account. What you studied and the sites you view are not recorded.
(g) SK's Toolbox: the data and settings of Clock, Calc, Memo, Todo, Countdown, Timetable, and Roulette are stored only in the browser and are not sent. However, when you connect SK's Toolbox to your SK Hub Systems account and start online sync, the data of the apps you choose is encrypted on your device with a sync passphrase you choose and then stored in your account. The decryption key exists only on your devices, so the Developer cannot read the contents. Memo images are not sent. In addition, when the Developer shares a timetable with Timetable, the email addresses entered by the Developer as recipients and the shared timetable are stored in SK Hub Systems and used only to show the timetable to the person signed in with the SK Hub Systems account for that email address. Recipients cannot see each other, and the data is deleted when sharing stops. If you join a share with an invite code, or open a timetable shared with you, your name, icon, email address, and the time you joined are stored in SK Hub Systems and used only so that the Developer who shares the timetable can see and remove members (other members cannot see them). When you open SK's Toolbox as a LINE MINI App, your LINE profile (name and icon) is used only for display on screen and is not stored or sent. "Send with LINE" sends content through LINE only to the people you choose, when you choose to.

Article 3 Information Handled by the Access Check
1. The Site (including SK's Lab tools), MALU, Nagi, and the Y-FILTER. management console perform an access check to respond to abuse and understand usage.
2. Stored in the browser: a support ID (a random identifier created for each browser), the terms consent status, and whether notices have been shown
3. Sent to and stored in SK Hub Systems: the support ID, the URL of the page accessed (excluding anything after "?"), the domain of the referring site, the browser type (user agent), language setting, date and time of access, and the result of the access restriction check
4. The access check does not record names, email addresses, or IP addresses.
5. Nothing is sent to the server until you choose "Agree and close," if you choose "Decline," or if you withdraw your agreement.
6. While signed in with an SK Hub Systems account, the support ID and time zone of that browser are also stored as the account's usage records (SK Hub Systems Account Terms, Articles 3 and 4).

Article 4 Information Handled for Inquiries
1. The following information sent from the contact form (https://sk-hub.jp/contact/) is stored in SK Hub Systems:
(a) The type of inquiry, the service concerned, and the message
(b) The reply-to email address (only if entered)
(c) The support ID, browser type (user agent), and language setting (only if "Include my support ID and browser info" is chosen)
(d) The date and time sent
2. This information is used only to respond to inquiries. Only the Developer can read it.

Article 4-2 Access Analytics (Google Analytics)
1. On the Site (including SK's Lab tools), MALU, and Nagi, access analytics using Google Analytics by Google LLC is performed to understand how the Services are used and improve them. It is performed only if you agree with analytics left on in the notice shown on your first visit (the switch in that notice is on by default if your browser's time zone is Japan, and off by default otherwise), or choose "Allow" in the notice shown. Until you choose, or if you don't allow it, Google Analytics is not loaded.
2. If you allow it, Google Analytics uses cookies and sends Google information such as the URL and title of pages viewed, the referring page, time spent, screen size, browser and OS type, language, and approximate region (country or region level). IP addresses are anonymized by Google Analytics, and the Developer does not receive this information in a form that identifies individuals.
3. The Developer does not use Google signals or advertising features. The SK Hub Systems account page, developer pages, and consent screens are not included in access analytics. MALU search terms are not sent.
4. You can withdraw your permission at any time from "Analytics settings" at the bottom of the Site. After withdrawal, no further measurement is made, and Google Analytics cookies in the browser are deleted.
5. For how Google handles information, see Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Article 4-3 Google User Data (Signing In with a Google Account)
1. When you sign in to an SK Hub Systems Account with a Google Account, the Developer receives only the following information from Google (Google Sign-In requests only the openid, email, and profile scopes):
(a) Your Google Account identifier
(b) Your name
(c) Your email address
(d) The URL of your profile picture
2. The Developer does not access data from any other Google services, such as Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, or Contacts, and does not receive your Google Account password.
3. The information in paragraph 1 is used only for the following purposes:
(a) Creating your SK Hub Systems Account and signing you in (verifying that it is you)
(b) Showing your name and icon on the account page and in the site header
(c) Linking the data you choose to save (settings sync, MALU, Nagi, SK's Toolbox, etc.) to your own account
(d) In SK's Toolbox Timetable, matching an email address entered as a recipient with the email address of the signed-in user, so that the timetable is shown to that person
(e) Delivering account notices (such as replies to your inquiries) to you
4. The information in paragraph 1 is stored in Firebase by Google LLC (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore; Cloud Firestore data is stored in the Tokyo region). All communication is encrypted with HTTPS.
5. The Developer does not sell information received from Google, does not use it for advertising or marketing, and does not provide it to third parties. It is not used to develop or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Except for processing by Google LLC as the provider of Firebase and cases required by law (Article 8), it is not passed to anyone else. The Developer looks at the content only to respond to failures or misuse, to respond to legal requests, or at your request.
6. The information in paragraph 1 is kept until you delete your SK Hub Systems Account. At https://sk-hub.jp/account/, you can download the stored information and delete your account at any time. When you delete your account, the account information, including the information in paragraph 1, is deleted. You can also remove the connection to SK Hub Systems in your Google Account settings (https://myaccount.google.com/connections). The Developer may delete accounts that have not been signed in to for two years or more.
7. The Developer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

Article 5 Purposes of Use
1. Providing, maintaining, and improving the Services
2. Responding to abuse and nuisance behavior (including access restrictions)
3. Responding to inquiries
4. Important notices

Article 6 Storage Location and Security
1. Information stored in SK Hub Systems is stored in Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) by Google LLC. Cloud Firestore data is stored in the Tokyo region.
2. Communications are encrypted with HTTPS. Only the Developer can read access check logs and the content of inquiries.

Article 7 Retention Period
1. Access check logs are deleted progressively once more than one year has passed since they were stored.
2. Access restriction information is deleted when the restriction is lifted.
3. The content of inquiries is deleted about one year after they have been handled.
4. Where Service Terms specify a retention period, that period applies.
5. Google Analytics data older than 2 months is deleted according to the Google Analytics settings.

Article 8 Provision to Third Parties
The Developer does not provide the information it handles to third parties, except as required by law.

Article 9 External Services
The Services use the following external services. These services may receive IP addresses and the like when accessed. Their handling follows each company's privacy policy.
(a) GitHub Pages (hosting the Site; GitHub, Inc.)
(b) Firebase (SK Hub Systems; Google LLC)
(c) Google Fonts (displaying text; Google LLC)
(d) Google Analytics (access analytics, Google LLC; only if allowed, as set out in Article 4-2)
(e) Cloudflare Workers and D1 (signing in with LINE, Kakao, or a passkey; Cloudflare, Inc.)
(f) LINE Login (LY Corporation) and Kakao Login (Kakao Corp.) (only when signing in with each)

Article 10 Information of Minors
The Services are expected to be used by minors as well. The Developer strives not to handle more information than necessary.

Article 11 Viewing and Deleting Information
1. Clearing the browser's site data deletes the information stored in the browser.
2. SK Hub Systems account information can be downloaded and deleted on the account page (https://sk-hub.jp/account/).
3. To request viewing or deletion of any other information stored on the server, please contact us through the contact page (https://sk-hub.jp/contact/), including your support ID (shown on the Site's support page).

Article 11-2 Residents of the EEA, the UK, and Similar Regions
1. For residents of regions where the GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation) or similar laws apply, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Developer (contact: https://sk-hub.jp/contact/) is the controller of the information and handles it on the following legal bases.
(a) Access check (Article 3): the Developer's legitimate interest in providing the Services securely and preventing misuse
(b) Access analytics (Article 4-2): your consent
(c) Inquiries (Article 4): the Developer's legitimate interest in responding to your request
(d) SK Hub Systems account and SK Hub Systems: providing the services under their respective terms
2. In accordance with applicable law, these users may request access to, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and portability of their information, and may object to its processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing before withdrawal). Requests are accepted by the method in Article 11.
3. These users may lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in their region.
4. Information in SK Hub Systems is stored in Japan (the Tokyo region of Cloud Firestore). Japan is recognized by the European Commission and the United Kingdom as ensuring an adequate level of protection for personal data. With Firebase Authentication, Google Analytics, and the external services in Article 9, information may be processed outside Japan, such as in the United States, and each company takes safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Article 12 Changes
If this Policy is changed, notice will be posted on the Site. For important changes, such as increasing the information handled, consent will be requested again.
