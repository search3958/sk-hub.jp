Article 4 How the Feature Works
1. From the management console, Administrators can send settings to one or more devices, start learning mode, copy another device's settings, disconnect devices, and so on.
2. Devices check for sent settings about every 2 minutes and apply any new sent settings. Applied settings take priority over changes made on the device's settings screen.
3. Devices report their current settings and status to Administrators when settings change and about every 10 minutes.
4. Users can send requests to Administrators from the block page of a device connected to SK Hub Systems. Approving a site request adds that site's domain to the device's allow list. When the daily usage limit is reached, Users can request an extension (15, 30, or 60 minutes), and approving it extends the limit for that day only. The result (approved or not) is notified to the device.
5. If a daily usage limit is set, the device counts the time spent viewing web pages in 1-minute units (time with no activity and no sound for a certain period is not counted) and restricts access for the rest of the day once the limit is reached.
6. Like the Owner, Co-admins can send settings to, reply to requests from, and disconnect all devices in the management group.
7. Applying sent settings or reporting may be delayed or may not happen depending on the network, the device's power and sleep state, the state of the browser, and the like.
8. A personal device saves a backup when its settings change (at most once every 10 minutes) and when the User chooses to. The User can restore the device's settings from backups in the same account. Requests under paragraph 4 cannot be sent from a personal device.

Article 5 Information Handled
1. The information sent from devices and stored is as follows:
(a) Software settings (block rules, block keywords, categories, allow list, time restrictions, ad blocking, download and upload restrictions, SafeSearch, the settings screen access code (converted into an irreversible form; the code itself is not sent), block page design, Newtab settings, and so on)
(b) Status (last connected date and time, the number of the applied sent settings, and usage time (minutes) and limits for that day and for each of the last 7 days)
(c) Device information (Software version, browser user agent, and OS type)
(d) Only on Chromebooks where the "Send device information to the administrator" setting is enabled: memory usage, the device's asset ID (only for devices managed by an organization), and the email address of the account signed in to the browser
(e) Connection information (device name, date and time of connection, the pairing code used, and an anonymous device ID issued automatically for each device)
(f) Only when a User sends a request: the URL and domain of the page, the blocked category, the message entered by the User, and the date and time sent (for usage time extension requests, the desired number of minutes and the date)
(g) Only for personal devices: backups (the settings in (a) of this paragraph, the device name, the version of the Software, and the date and time saved)
2. The information stored about Administrators is the Google account identifier, email address, and display name (used for sign-in), the pairing codes and invite codes issued, and the email address of the Administrator who replied to a request. The email addresses and display names of management group members are shown to the Administrators of the same management group.
3. The Feature does not send or store browsing history, search terms, the contents of pages viewed, records of blocked pages, input, or location information (except content a User sends themselves in a request under paragraph 1 (f) of this Article).
4. This information is used only to provide the Feature (sending settings, reports, requests, and device management). It is never used for advertising, sale, provision to third parties, or analysis of Users' behavior.
5. All devices using the Software obtain the blocklist from the Developer's servers (Article 6) without signing in. No information identifying the User is sent when obtaining it. The blocklist is managed by the Developer and cannot be edited by Administrators or Users.
